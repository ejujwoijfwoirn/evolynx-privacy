Privacy Policy — EvoLynx

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how EvoLynx ("the App") handles information when you use it.

Information We Collect

EvoLynx does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The App does not require an account, login, or any form of registration. All game progress (your creatures, resources, achievements, and settings) is saved locally on your device only, and is never sent to us or to any third party.

Permissions

  • Vibration — used for in-game haptic feedback during gameplay (e.g. combat impacts).
  • Notifications — used only to schedule local reminders on your own device (e.g. "your daily reward is ready"). These notifications are generated and scheduled entirely on your device and are never sent from or reported to any server.

No other device permissions (location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage, etc.) are requested or used.

Third-Party Services

EvoLynx does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-collection SDKs. No data about you or your device is shared with any third party, because none is collected in the first place.

Children's Privacy

EvoLynx does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it collects no information from any user.

Data Deletion

Since all data is stored only on your device, you can remove all app data at any time via your device's Settings → Apps → EvoLynx → Clear Data, or by using the "Erase Save Data" option in the App's own Settings menu. Uninstalling the App removes all of its locally stored data.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a future update adds an online feature), this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
